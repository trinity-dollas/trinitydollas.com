Booking & cancellation
  • Your deposit is non-refundable and comes off the total cost of your session.
  • Please give at least 3 days (72 hours) notice to cancel or reschedule. Your deposit then moves to your new date.
  • Less than 3 days' notice, or a no-show, means the deposit is forfeited.
  • Running more than 20 minutes late without letting me know may mean losing your deposit and appointment.
  • Not ready to rebook? With 72 hours' notice you'll get a gift voucher instead.
  • Multiple reschedules may need a new deposit.
